About

A digital humanities project exploring the spatial and sensory imagination of the 18th-century English novel. The corpus draws on Project Gutenberg texts and a purpose-built gazetteer of 73 London and Bath venues active between roughly 1660 and 1820.

Evidence is assembled from heterogeneous sources — fiction, personal diary, topographical survey, poetry, letters — without treating any one as foundational. Where the sources diverge, that divergence is the scholarship.
